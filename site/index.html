Why weavN

One conversation. Two faces of the truth.

Chat and tickets have always been two tools fighting over the same work. weavN makes them one surface, kept in sync both ways — so nothing is copied, re-typed, or lost between them.

Bidirectional truth

Send a message, it becomes a JIRA issue or comment. Move a ticket in JIRA, it updates in chat. There is no "integration lag" because there is no copy — chat and JIRA are two views of exactly the same records.

Channels are Boards

A channel is a filtered JIRA board; a thread is an issue; a reply is a comment. The shapes line up one-to-one, so every conversation gets a real lifecycle.

Real-time sync

Messages, edits, reactions, read state and status changes propagate live in both directions — the moment they happen, on either side.

Encrypted DMs

Direct messages live in an encrypted vault, separate from the ticket store — private conversations stay private, team work stays on the board.

SSO on day one

Sign in with your identity provider over OIDC. Every message is written to JIRA as the real person who sent it — authorship and permissions carry through.

Agile, built in

Sprints and backlog refinement are first-class in the chat interface — groom and plan where the conversation already is, not in a separate tool.

Rust, end to end

Backend and client are written in Rust — fast, memory-safe, and lean. weavN adds a thin, auditable projection layer on top of your JIRA; it is not another database quietly becoming your source of truth.

How it works

Connected in minutes, not a migration.

weavN connects to the JIRA your team already runs. Nothing moves; your existing projects simply gain a chat face.

Connect your JIRA

Sign in through your identity provider and authorize weavN against your JIRA site. Permissions and identities come straight from your org.

Discover & backfill

On first connect, weavN discovers your projects and does a bounded backfill, so your channels open with real history — not an empty room.

Just chat

Talk like it's Slack. Every top-level message becomes an issue, every reply a comment, every channel a board — kept in sync, both ways, forever.

The mapping

Slack and JIRA are the same shape.

A Slack thread is two levels deep: a root message and a flat list of replies. A JIRA issue is two levels deep: the issue and a flat list of comments. weavN snaps them together with no nesting tricks.

  • Channel ↔ Board — a filtered view over issues stamped with a stable channel ID.
  • Thread ↔ Issue — summary is the first line, body is the message.
  • Reply ↔ Comment — a flat, chronological list, exactly like chat.
  • Person ↔ JIRA user — authorship and permissions are never faked.
